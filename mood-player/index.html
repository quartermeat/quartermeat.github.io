Mood Player is part of Digital Terrarium, a desktop project that responds to machine activity. CPU load, network traffic, and memory pressure select a mood; your configured Spotify playlists provide the music.

As a playing track nears its end, the local companion queues the next selection. Playback control requires Spotify Premium and authorization in the desktop companion.

Your music connection stays local.

This page introduces the project. Spotify sign-in runs through the companion on your own computer, where its authorization token is stored. This website does not ask for Spotify credentials.

The mood comes from workstation telemetry. It does not analyze your audio or infer your emotional state.
