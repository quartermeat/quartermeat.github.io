How it works

Planning ends. Work continues.

A person or model chooses the goal and helps with exceptions. Deterministic scripts run the routine work on the game clock, using the world as the source of truth.

  1. 01PersonGives intent, boundaries and authority for consequential work.
  2. 02PlannerMaps an unfamiliar goal to known capabilities and handles exceptions.
  3. 03AutomatonPursues one explicit outcome through conditions, loops and smaller automata.
  4. 04SkillsFind, walk, mine, carry, place, connect and verify.
  5. 05BodyEnforces position, reach, inventory, health and the passage of time.
  6. 06WorldSupplies the authoritative state and evidence that the outcome exists.

Directives are automaton scripts

Small jobs become a larger purpose.

Each directive has a legible outcome. Conditions and jumps keep it moving; includes compose reliable jobs into larger ones. When work cannot continue, Crew stops and reports the smallest missing decision or resource.

Working today

A real body with useful hands.

Crewmate combines a Factorio mod with a Go bridge. You can direct Crew from game chat, the command line or any MCP-capable agent.

◎

Walk and observe

Navigate with the game pathfinder, inspect nearby entities and report current state.

◇

Gather resources

Find and hand-mine coal, iron, copper, stone and wood at game-paced speed.

▦

Build from blueprints

Find a valid site, place ghosts, carry materials and build what is reachable.

↳

Use parameters, conditions, labels, jumps and includes to assemble repeatable work.

Fair by construction

A body, not a cursor.

Crew is a Factorio character standing beside you. The physical constraints are what make its help understandable: you can see the route, inspect the inventory, interrupt the job and judge the result in the factory.

  • No cheat mode or indestructible flag
  • No teleporting where walking would do
  • No items or buildings created from nothing
  • No hidden redesigns of the player’s base
  • Long work announces progress
  • Failures return structured evidence
